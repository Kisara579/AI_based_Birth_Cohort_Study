About Dataset
This dataset is designed to facilitate the development and evaluation of machine learning models for predicting the risk and potential characteristics of epilepsy. It compiles a rich set of features, including patient history, demographic information, and clinical indicators, to provide a comprehensive foundation for predictive analysis.

Key Features:

Gender:Patient's gender.
Age_Onset_Symptoms:Age at which initial symptoms appeared.
Socioeconomic_Status: A categorical or numerical representation of the patient's socioeconomic background.
History_Birth_Asphyxia: Indicates a history of birth asphyxia.
History_Premature_Birth: Indicates a history of premature birth.
History_Low_Birth_Weight: Indicates a history of low birth weight.
History_Family_Epilepsy: Indicates a family history of epilepsy.
History_Cerebral_Malaria: Indicates a history of cerebral malaria.
History_Meningitis_Encephalitis: Indicates a history of meningitis or encephalitis.
History_Severe_Head_Trauma: Indicates a history of severe head trauma.
Intellectual_Disability: Indicates the presence of intellectual disability.
Autism_Spectrum_Disorder: Indicates the presence of autism spectrum disorder.
Imaging_Abnormalities: Indicates abnormalities found during medical imaging.
EEG_Abnormalities_Baseline: Indicates abnormalities in baseline EEG readings.
History_Stroke_Brain_Tumor: Indicates a history of stroke or brain tumor.
Calculated_Risk_Score: A pre-calculated risk score based on various factors.
Predicted_Prob_Epilepsy: The predicted probability of epilepsy.
Seizure_Type_if_Diagnosed: The specific type of seizure if a diagnosis is present.
Epilepsy_Diagnosis: The target variable
Potential Uses:

This dataset can be used by researchers and data scientists to:

Build and benchmark classification models to predict epilepsy risk.
Explore correlations between clinical history and the likelihood of developing epilepsy.
Develop tools for early risk stratification in clinical settings.
Analyze the impact of different features on the final risk score.
Design predictive models for identifying specific seizure types.
